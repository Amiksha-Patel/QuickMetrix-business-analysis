# QuickMetrix — Business Analysis Case Study

## Project Overview
This project explores the business analysis lifecycle for a customer experience and online reputation management platform. It focuses on understanding business needs, documenting requirements, modelling workflows, and planning product enhancements.

**Project Type:** Business Analysis Certification / Upskilling Case Study  
**Product Domain:** Customer Experience Management (CXM) and Online Reputation Management (ORM)  
**Delivery Approaches:** Waterfall (V1.0) and Agile (V2.0)  
**My Background:** ORM Analyst with Business Analysis certification

## Business Problem
Organisations receive customer feedback and complaints across multiple digital channels. Managing these interactions, tracking their status, identifying sentiment trends, and reporting outcomes can become challenging without a unified workflow.

This case study examines how a centralised platform can support complaint management, response tracking, reporting, and future product enhancements.

## Project Objectives
- Define the business problem, objectives, scope, and stakeholders.
- Document business, functional, and non-functional requirements.
- Model As-Is and To-Be business workflows.
- Develop user stories and acceptance criteria for proposed enhancements.
- Create a requirements traceability matrix and UAT scenarios.

## Project Scope

### V1.0 — Core Platform
- User access and role management
- Unified inbox and interaction filtering
- Sentiment and tag management
- Manual ticket creation, assignment, and status tracking
- Multi-channel responses
- Reporting and publishing workflows

### V2.0 — Proposed Enhancements
- AI chatbot for approved brand and platform resources
- Enhanced customer relationship categorisation
- Mobile-browser experience improvements
- Influencer management
- Additional integrations and Help Center capabilities

*Note: V2.0 items are proposed enhancements for this case study, not claims about existing product functionality.*

## Business Analysis Deliverables
- Project overview and scope
- Stakeholder analysis and elicitation plan
- Business and functional requirements
- Non-functional requirements and business rules
- As-Is and To-Be process diagrams
- Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
- Agile user stories and acceptance criteria
- Sample backlog and sprint planning
- UAT scenarios and acceptance approach

## Methodology
The case study uses a Waterfall-style approach to structure the V1.0 requirements and an Agile approach to plan proposed V2.0 enhancements.
