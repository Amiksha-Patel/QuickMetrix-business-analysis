# QuickMetrix — Project Overview

## 1. Project Background

QuickMetrix is the product used as the basis for this Business Analysis case study. The project focuses on a platform for managing customer interactions across digital channels, supporting online reputation management (ORM), tracking customer complaints, and analysing customer sentiment.

This case study explores how business analysis techniques can be applied to define platform requirements and plan future enhancements.

## 2. Business Problem

Businesses may receive customer feedback, questions, and complaints across multiple digital channels. When these interactions are difficult to manage in one place, teams may face challenges with tracking ticket status, coordinating responses, monitoring sentiment, and preparing performance reports.

The case study examines how a unified workflow could help teams manage these activities more consistently.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Define business needs, objectives, and project boundaries.
- Identify stakeholders and their information needs.
- Document business, functional, and non-functional requirements.
- Model relevant business processes and workflows.
- Plan proposed product enhancements using Agile practices.
- Define requirements traceability and user acceptance testing (UAT) scenarios.

## 4. Project Approach

### V1.0 — Core Requirements

A Waterfall-style approach will be used to organise the initial scope, analyse requirements, document expected system behaviour, and define validation criteria.

### V2.0 — Proposed Enhancements

An Agile approach will be used to organise proposed enhancements into user stories, acceptance criteria, and a sample sprint backlog.

## 5. Scope

### In Scope — V1.0 Case Study

- User login and role-based access
- Unified inbox and interaction filtering
- Sentiment and tag management
- Manual ticket creation, assignment, and status tracking
- Responses across supported channels
- Reporting and publishing workflows
- Crisis management and canned responses

### Proposed for V2.0

- AI chatbot for approved platform and brand resources
- Customer relationship categorisation
- Improved mobile-browser experience
- Influencer management
- Additional integrations
- Help Center capabilities
- Interface customisation options

### Out of Scope

- Native mobile application development
- AI-based ticket assignment
- AI chatbot implementation within V1.0
- CRM enhancements within V1.0

## 6. Stakeholders

The following stakeholder groups are proposed for analysis. Their responsibilities and involvement will be refined during stakeholder analysis.

| Stakeholder | Potential interest |
|---|---|
| Product Owner / Sponsor | Business objectives and product value |
| Project Manager | Schedule, coordination, and delivery |
| ORM Analyst | Monitoring interactions and managing tickets |
| ORM Team Lead / Manager | Team oversight and reporting |
| Customer Care Agent | Responding to customer interactions |
| Administrator | User access and platform configuration |
| Development Team | Technical implementation |
| QA / Testing Team | Requirement verification and testing |

## 7. Key Assumptions

- The platform supports multiple digital interaction channels.
- Users require a central place to review and manage customer interactions.
- Ticket assignment and status changes in V1.0 are performed manually.
- V2.0 features are proposed enhancements for this case study.
- Product behaviour and technical constraints will need further validation before requirements are finalised.
